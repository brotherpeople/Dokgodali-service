# Dokgodali (독고달이)

**Language:** English | [한국어](README.ko.md)

Your moving helper on Germany's Autobahn — a service prototype spanning everything from moving quotes to crew schedule management.

## 1. Quote & Booking Flow

A customer-facing flow that walks through volume calculation → route & options → customer info and the official quote (PDF).

**[Test it here →](https://brotherpeople.github.io/Dokgodali-service/dokgodali-quote-flow-en.html)**
**[한국어 버전 →](https://brotherpeople.github.io/Dokgodali-service/dokgodali-quote-flow.html)**

## 2. Crew Schedule Management

Once an admin registers a confirmed job and assigns crew, each crew member can check their own schedule and availability for the day.

**[Test it here →](https://brotherpeople.github.io/Dokgodali-service/dokgodali-schedule-en.html)**
**[한국어 버전 →](https://brotherpeople.github.io/Dokgodali-service/dokgodali-schedule.html)**

## 3. Small Package Delivery Booking

A customer-facing booking flow for sending small boxes/cargo from Germany to Korea — enter the item size/quantity and delivery address, then choose a pickup/drop-off method and personal info step by step.

**[Test it here →](https://brotherpeople.github.io/Dokgodali-service/dokgodali-delivery.html)**
